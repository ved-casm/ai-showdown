Brief to result

Invented brief, written the way a marketing partner would send it.

Brief (fictional)

Objective: lift add-to-cart on the ORRIN No. 7 PDP and give paid social 3 ad angles.
Audience: 25-40, buys fragrance online without smelling it first, cares about look and gifting.
Placements: PDP hero (16:9 desktop, 4:5 mobile), 1:1 and 9:16 social, wide banner.
Must hold: frosted glass, black cap, ORRIN No. 7 label text.

Decisions I made

  1. Bright bathroom shelf for the PDP hero: clean, label reads, shape is clear.
  2. Warm lamp-lit dresser for lifestyle: mood and gifting, not specs.
  3. Macro of the label and cap for the third ad: proves the finish.
tools: personal agent image tool (edit from reference) / steps: stock photo, brand label, 3 scenes

Edit control

Steps from one source image. Each step names what was wrong and what I asked for.

1 reference: branded source photo
2 base: reference placed in a new room
3a problem: a real book title (THE DAILY STOIC) is readable on the dresser
3b inpaint fix: cover blanked, rest unchanged
4 outpaint: widened to 21:9 with room for text
5 img2img restyle: same scene at dusk
prompts: edit only the named element, keep everything else identical / extend sideways to 21:9, keep the product as is / restyle to blue hour, keep composition. Honest note: In the outpaint the bottle came out much smaller than in the source, so I would re-run for a banner; I kept it to show the real result.

Repeatability

Same prompt and same reference, run 4 times.

run 1: shape and label hold
run 2: shape and label hold
run 3: shape and label hold
run 4: shape and label hold
Template

Place this exact [product], with the same [pattern], proportions and [label], on [scene], [light], photorealistic commercial photo, [focal rule]. Reference: branded source image. Keep the reference fixed, change only the bracket parts.

Result: label readable in 4 of 4 (small in the wide frame, clear at full size), bottle shape and cap in 4 of 4. Fix for a real project: keep the macro label shot as the second reference so the text stays sharp in wide frames.
