five tool examples

Tool judgment

Five submitted frames for the same ORRIN No.7 bathroom-shelf brief. Compare the label, bottle, light and framing. Tool names follow the submitted labels; model versions and settings are not recorded here.

Shared prompt

A phone-shot style photo of a small frosted glass perfume bottle with a black cap and a plain white label that says ORRIN No.7, sitting on a bathroom shelf next to a toothbrush cup, soft window light from the left, slightly uneven framing, natural shadows, no studio gloss, 4:5 vertical.
